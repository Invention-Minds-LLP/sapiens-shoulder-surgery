Hand to Shoulder Treatment in Bangalore
Hand to shoulder treatment focuses on diagnosing and treating problems affecting the hand, wrist, elbow, and shoulder joints. These conditions often cause pain, stiffness, weakness, numbness, or limited arm movement.
Treatment options include non-surgical treatment, medication, physiotherapy,
injections and when needed, hand to shoulder surgery. Surgery is recommended only after careful evaluation and when conservative treatment does not give relief.
